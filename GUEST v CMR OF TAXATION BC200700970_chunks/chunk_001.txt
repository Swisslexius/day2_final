purposes taken out in 1987 — cessation of involvement in business in 1991 — taxpayer only liable for 
principal and interest if two early repayments not made on time. EVIDENCE — Evidence Act 1995 (Cth) s 69 — “person” not required to be identified. WORDS AND PHRASES — “records” “proceeding”
