Was the interest liability incurred by him in carrying on a business for the purpose of gaining or producing 
assessable income? Issue 1 — loss or outgoing “incurred”? [66]  By reason of the failure to pay the two $5000 instalments on time Mr Guest became legally liable for 
repayment of the full amount of the loan and interest thereon. The interest which accrued under the loan agreement 
during the relevant years was therefore “incurred” by him. In those years they were presently existing liabilities: 
Nilsen Development Laboratories Pty Ltd v Federal Commissioner of Taxation (1981) 144 CLR 616 at 627.
