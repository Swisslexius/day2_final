In R & D there is the qualification that the business in question, the leasing of an office building, was still carried on, 
although not by the taxpayer but by a mortgagee in possession. [74]  In the present case the Commissioner argues that some intervening event or act has broken the nexus 
between the income-obtaining activity and the outgoing in question. Such a breaking of the nexus is said to have 
occurred when Mr Guest
• failed to repay the loan on the due date (31 March 1992), or
• failed to accept the Receivers’ offer in August 1996
The Commissioner also says the lapse of time in itself is sufficient to break the nexus. [75]  The concept of a break in the nexus was applied in Commissioner of Taxation v Riverside Rd Lodge Pty Ltd 
(in liq) (1990) 23 FCR 305. In 1970 the taxpayer company borrowed money to acquire land and construct a motel.
