[5]  The joint venture deed. Mr Guest and the other individuals agreed with Corindi Blueberry Growers Pty Ltd to 
enter into a joint venture for the production of blueberries on land owned by the latter. Mr and Mrs Guest each had a 
one-sixth share and the other two investors one-third each. Corindi Blueberry Growers granted to Mr Guest and the 
other individuals a non-exclusive licence to occupy its land for the purpose of blueberry production. Five hundred 
trees were to be allocated to Mr Guest and the other individuals (the project as a whole involved hundreds of other 
investors).
